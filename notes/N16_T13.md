Not a 2 Gvox/s claim. Not 3090 Ti. Kernel not removed.

- T14 2.16 phases cumulative vcount=177.82 ms of WS=1343.79
- Deep (T2T5, no T14) 2.16a vcount=177.84 ms of WS=1312.02

`k_count_v2` `atomicAdd(&vcount[parent[i]], 1)` over the full 720M-voxel slab. BFS `qsz[p] = vcount[plat_begin[p]]` is **voxel occupancy of the plateau**, not corner-run length (`ws.cu` k_indep_bfs: undersized qsz walks one plateau into the next and corrupts labels). After flatten, `parent[i]` is a voxel root id, so `vcount` is nvox-sized; it cannot shrink to nC without a dense remap.

Dropping the kernel is identity-illegal. A compact-nC histogram is a different experiment. 178 ms off 1312 is not a 1.2x vs T2T5 (need ≤1092, i.e. -220 ms).
